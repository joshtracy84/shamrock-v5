Shamrock V5 privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026.

Shamrock V5 is a personal tool used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's Google Drive solely to read and write the owner's own notes.

  • No data is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.
  • No data is used for advertising.
  • Google user data is stored only in the owner's own Drive and on the owner's own computer.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Shamrock V5's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
